This privacy policy explains what information the Islamic ilm Quiz app ("the App") handles. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Data the App stores

Islamic ilm Quiz is offline-first and requires no account, no sign-in, and no personal information to use.

The App stores only the following on your own device (never sent to us or anyone else):

  • Quiz progress, scores, and daily-challenge streaks
  • Reminder times and notification preferences
  • App settings (e.g. whether onboarding was completed)

This data lives in the App's private on-device storage. Uninstalling the App deletes it. We operate no servers and receive no data from the App.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to keep the App free. AdMob may collect and use:

  • Device or other identifiers — such as the device advertising ID and device identifiers
  • Technical and usage data needed for ad serving, ad measurement, and fraud prevention

This data is collected and processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising privacy practices.

You can reset or limit ad personalization on your device:

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or Opt out of Ads Personalization, depending on your Android version).

We do not control Google's data practices; questions about them should be directed to Google.

3. Children's privacy

The App is a general-audience Islamic learning app intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We knowingly collect no personal data from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with the App, contact us at sahibzadaalyansami@gmail.com and we will address it.

4. Permissions used

The App requests only the permissions needed for its stated features:

  • Notifications — to deliver daily quiz, Dua of the Day, and adhkar reminders.
  • Exact alarms — to fire reminders at the times you choose.
  • Vibration — for haptic feedback when answering quiz questions.

These permissions are used solely on-device for the features described above.

5. Data sharing

We share no data ourselves, because we receive none. The only third party that receives data from the App is Google (AdMob), as described in Section 2, for advertising purposes.

6. Data retention and security

Because the App stores your preferences only on your device and transmits nothing to us, there is nothing for us to retain or secure on servers. On-device data is protected by your device's own lock and storage protections.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same URL with a new effective date. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
